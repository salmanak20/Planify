# Planify: Product Requirements Document ## App Overview Planify is an all-in-one productivity Android app that seamlessly integrates notes, tasks, a calendar, and a collaborative workspace. The goal is to provide a single destination for personal and professional organization. ## Target Audience - Professionals managing complex projects - Students organizing study schedules and notes - Anyone looking for a unified tool to replace multiple productivity apps ## Proposed Screens 1. **Dashboard (The "Command Center"):** A high-level overview of the user's day. Includes a "Next Up" task section, upcoming calendar events, and a quick-access area for recently edited notes or active workspaces. 2. **Unified Calendar View:** A versatile calendar (day/week/month views) that shows both scheduled events and time-blocked tasks. Users should be able to drag and drop tasks onto the calendar. 3. **Task Manager (List & Kanban):** A dedicated space for managing tasks and projects. Includes features like nesting (sub-tasks), priorities, tags, and the ability to toggle between a standard list view and a Kanban board. 4. **Notes & Knowledge Base:** A rich-text editor for taking notes. Features include folder organization, tagging, and the ability to link notes to specific tasks or calendar events. 5. **Workspace Collaboration Hub:** A view for shared projects. Shows team activity, shared task lists, and a centralized area for workspace-specific notes and files. ## Visual Direction - **Modern & Clean:** Focus on white space and clear typography to reduce cognitive load. - **Android Native Feel:** Adhere to Material Design 3 principles (Material You) for a familiar and intuitive experience. - **Color Coding:** Use subtle color coding to distinguish between notes, tasks, and calendar entries.
